SENTINEL COMMUNICATIONS DIRECTORATE

After completing his National Service as a Signals Operator, Cyrus began looking for work in communications.
He wanted something familiar. Something that would allow him to put the skills he had spent the past two years learning to use.
A few weeks later, he found a position with the Sentinel Communications Directorate (SCD).

The SCD operates a network of communications stations across remote areas, where operators monitor radio traffic and maintain contact with central command.
The work is mostly routine.
Monitor assigned frequencies.
Record relevant transmissions.
Report anything important.

For his first assignment, Cyrus was posted to Station 07, one of the SCD's remote communications stations.
His shift would run from 2300 to 0600.
